About me

A bit about how I work.

I enjoy finding the story inside a dataset: cleaning it, asking the right questions, and presenting findings in a way that helps people act on them.

My toolkit covers the full analysis workflow, from querying data in SQL and cleaning it in Excel or Python, to building interactive dashboards in Power BI and Tableau. I'm currently building hands-on projects that apply these skills to real business problems.
